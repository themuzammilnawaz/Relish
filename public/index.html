Our Story

A table set with
quiet intention

Relish began with a simple belief: that Bahawalpur deserved a table as considered as any in Lahore or Karachi. Where the dough rests, the steaks are cut by hand, and the evening is built around you.

Founder Abdul Hanan built the kitchen around three things — patience, provenance, and precision. The menu reads like a story: familiar enough to comfort, refined enough to surprise.
